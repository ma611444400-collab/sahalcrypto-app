Adeegyo Ku Salaysan Amni iyo Xawaare

Sahal Crypto maahan oo kaliya boorso, ee waa nidaam dhammaystiran oo xallinaya dhammaan baahiyahaaga cryptocurrency.

Amni aad u Sareeya (Security)

Nidaamka Sahal wuxuu adeegsadaa codeeynta amniga ugu sarraysa. Dhammaan xogahaaga gaarka ah waxay ku keydsan yihiin gudaha sanduuqa deegaanka ammaan ee taleefankaaga adoo adeegsanaya Room Database, iyadoo aan cid kale helgeli karin.

Xawaare Dhaqso Badan (Fast Transactions)

Diiwaangeli amaradaada iibka iyo iibsashada adigoo isticmaalaya screenshots-ka cadaymaha EVC Plus ama lacag-bixinta gacanta. Iska ilow dib-u-dhaca, nidaamkayagu wuxaa u shaqeeyaa si otomaatig ah oo ansixintu waa daqiiqado.

Taageero 24/7 Ah (Support Widget)

Kooxdayada caawinaadda ee Sahal Crypto waxay diyaar kuu yihiin maalin iyo habeenba. Si toos ah uga bilow wada hadalka app-ka dhexdiisa adoo adeegsanaya WhatsApp-ka kooxda maamulka si dhib kasta lagugu xalliyo si dhakhso ah.

Habka Dejinta Waa mid aad u Sahlan

Fadlan raac tilmaamahan kooban si aad ugu rakibato Sahal Crypto si ammaan ah taleefankaaga Android.

01

Deji Faylka APK

Guji badanka "Download APK" ee hoose ama sare si aad u bilowdo soo dejinta faylka rasmiga ah ee barnaamijka.

02

Oggolow Ilaha aan la aqoon (Unknown Sources)

Aad qaybta "Settings" ee taleefankaaga -> "Security" -> kisha oggolaanshaha "Install Unknown Apps" si uu ugu oggolaado browser-ka inuu ku rakibo APK.

03

Rakib oo ku Raaxayso Barnaamijka

Fura faylka aad soo dejisay oo guji "Install". Markuu dhameeyo fura, waxaadna diyaar u tay inaad gasho suuqa cryptocurrency si sahlan!
